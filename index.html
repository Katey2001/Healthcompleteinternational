Health starts with knowledge and opportunity

Health Complete International is a humanitarian, development-focused organisation. Its work reaches women, children, young people, persons with disabilities, older persons and low-income households, so that nobody is left behind.

A society empowered through knowledge, equitable opportunities and accessible interventions to realise optimal health, dignity and sustainable wellbeing.

Our work

Seven connected areas, because health, education, income and environment shape one another.

  • Health education

    Awareness on disease prevention, maternal and child health, and healthy living.

  • Healthcare access

    Free screenings, medical outreach and referrals to care.

  • Education

    Scholarships, mentorship and skills for disadvantaged young people.

  • Women and youth

    Training, entrepreneurship and financial literacy.

  • Environmental health

    Clean water, sanitation, hygiene and community clean-ups.

  • Social protection

    Support and inclusion for vulnerable households.

  • Partnerships

    Working with government, hospitals, companies and donors.
